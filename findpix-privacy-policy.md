# FindPix — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

FindPix ("the app") is made by BackspaceLabs. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

## The short version
- **Your photos, screenshots, notes and voice never leave your phone.** All reading, searching, face grouping and AI answers happen on your device.
- **We have no servers and no accounts.** We do not collect, store, sell or share your personal data.
- **No ads, no tracking, no analytics of our own.**

## What the app accesses on your phone
| Access | Why | Where it stays |
|---|---|---|
| Photos (read only) | To read text in your photos and screenshots, understand what is in them, and find faces, so you can search them | On your phone. Photos are never changed, deleted, copied off the phone or uploaded |
| Microphone | Only when you tap "Speak" to make a voice note | Turned into text on your phone (on-device speech recognition). Audio is not stored or sent |
| Camera | Only when you add a photo to a note | Saved inside the app on your phone |
| Notifications | To show progress while the app reads your photos in the background, with a Stop button | On your phone |

What the app learns from your photos (text, "meaning" numbers for search, face numbers for grouping, names you give) is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Face data is stored as numbers only; no face images are saved. This data is excluded from cloud backups.

## Internet use
The app uses the internet for one thing only: a one-time download of the on-device AI model file (from Hugging Face), when you choose to set up the Ask feature. Your photos, notes, questions and answers are never sent anywhere.

## Third-party components
The app uses Google ML Kit (on-device text recognition and face detection). Your photos are processed on your device and are not sent to Google. According to Google, ML Kit may send limited device, app and performance information (such as phone model, OS version, app version and performance metrics) to Google for diagnostics and usage analytics, encrypted in transit. See https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure

## Sharing
FindPix shares a photo only when **you** tap "Share", using your phone's own share menu. You choose where it goes.

## Deleting your data
Remove any item inside the app, or uninstall the app to delete everything it has stored. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

## Children
FindPix is not directed at children under 13.
